# YouTube channel candidates, round 2 — 2026-11-15

Owner request: find other high-subscriber Turkish channels that state a direction (yükseliş/düşüş) on instruments. Excluded on purpose: channels already tracked, and every channel the owner rejected on 2026-11-05 (`2026-11-05-youtube-channel-candidates.md`: QNB Invest, Tuncay Turşucu, Emre Kulcanay EKTV, Dikkat Boğa Çıkabilir, PARA ANALİZ, Nasıl Bir Ekonomi TV, Matriks Trader, Ekonomi Ekranı, İş Yatırım, KIDEMLİ ANALİST, EKOTÜRK, CNBC-e, Simge Fıstıkoğlu, Destek Yatırım).

Method: MTM/Marketing Türkiye "Bireysel Ekonomi Kanalları" rating report (Mar 2026), YouTube search, `yt-dlp` on the last 12 uploads per channel (uploads tab only, no live streams), Turkish auto-captions of the latest upload for the two best candidates. View counts are YouTube-rounded.

## Finding
Most large Turkish channels with explicit direction calls are already tracked (Devrim Akyıl, İslam Memiş, Kanal Finans, Atilla Yeşilada, Mesele Ekonomi, Murat Muratoğlu; Selçuk Geçer retired). The remaining pool is thin.

# US-market forecasters with a checkable track record — 2026-11-15

Owner request: international (US) channels/accounts that called Nasdaq/US corrections or analysed stocks well, and proved it more than once.

Method: web search for independent scorecards (Callsight, TrueAlpha, press scorecards), press-dated calls, `yt-dlp` on the last 12 uploads (subs, median views, titles). Note: the owner rejected the 2026-11-05 English list (David Lin Report, Benjamin Cowen, Kitco, Ciovacco, etc.); this round asks a different question (proven record), so only names with outside evidence are listed.

## Headline finding
- No one has an **independently audited** record of calling several Nasdaq corrections in advance. Long studies put guru accuracy near chance (CXO Advisory 2005–2012: 47.4% average; Bailey et al. 2017: ~48% over 6,627 forecasts).
- The 2026 test case: Nasdaq 100 peaked on **2026-10-06** and entered a correction in early November. I found **no named analyst who called it before the peak**; the public warnings came after the drop.
- "Called the top" claims on YouTube (e.g. Gareth Soloway, "major top is in", Kitco 2025-10-31) are self-reported; not verified.
